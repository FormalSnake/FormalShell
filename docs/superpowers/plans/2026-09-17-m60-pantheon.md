Where the shipped work deviates from the plan:

- Task 1b was added and was not in the original task list: pantheon's
  material needs highlight rims and a face gradient metamorphosis never
  used, and `Cell`, `Input`, `Switch` and `Track` hand-drew their own four
  layers rather than reading them off a `Box`, so those layers had nowhere
  to land. Every primitive but `FrameRing` (a `Shape`, not a `Box`) now
  composes one.
- The popover's gap off the bar stays at `barMargin` rather than the
  spec's own `0.5rem`: both recipes derive the same resting rect, and
  `emerge` only picks which shape draws it, not a second geometry input.
- The bar's translucent-dark cast and the ink's text-shadow blur are both
  dropped: the bar's window is exactly the band's own thickness, so there
  is no row under it for a cast to blur into, and the ink shadow draws as
  Qt's `Text.Raised` style, a flat offset rather than a blurred cast, to
  keep a `MultiEffect` off every bar glyph.
- `--panel-morph` and the screenshot legs needed rig fixes alongside the
  feature work: `--panel-morph` was reading the 1px line under a card's
  translucent fill rather than the fill's own lift (see Evidence below),
  and `screenshot region`/`full` gained a `processing` parameter, so
  `screenshot.sh` and `capture_edit.sh` now pass the empty string IPC
  already required by exact arity.
- The launcher's app grid needed its own fix, unrelated to any habit: the
  footer reveal read a row a tick after it left the view (see Evidence).
- The toast stack's frame `height` Behavior now guards on
  `stack.height > maxPeekLevels * peekOffset`, so a pile arriving from
  nothing snaps to its starting height instead of animating into it; the
  bubble's own unfold is what surfaced a pile rising behind its own
  entrance.
- `--notify-emerge` was added, a leg of its own rather than a rider on
  `--notify`: it rides `--pantheon` and reads the bubble's flip-in as a
  ladder down the card's own centre column, which `--notify`'s frame and
  `--deform`'s burst do not carry.

## Owner's ask (2026-09-17)

"A mode for the shell to be like pantheon, but made for my keyboard based
setup and tiling WMs." And, going to bed: "I want to wake up seeing the
pantheon style desktop." So the order below is by what is on screen first,
and the two habits that can wait (the switcher, the dark schedule) come
last and may land the next day without the rest waiting on them.

## Decisions

**P1 The table.** `shell/Theme/themes/pantheon.js`, every role transcribed
from the spec's material tables with the per-mode alphas as `{ light, dark }`
pairs. Light and dark differ in `highlight` (white, white 0.2) and in the
shadow levels; the layer lists are otherwise the same objects. Radii: 3 for
controls (`sm`), 6 for `md`, 9 for `xl`; the pantheon preset in `presets.js`
sets `radius: 6` so `Tokens.radiusTokens` lands on 2/4/6/10, and the table
pins the roles that elementary pins (`popover` 6, `card` and bubbles 9,
`tooltip` 3, `button` 3) by number rather than by step.

**P2 Habits.** `{ bar: "wingpanel", emerge: "popover", notification: "bubble",
launcher: "grid", switcher: true }`. Each of the first four is a second
QML file beside the existing one, chosen by a `Loader` on the habit;
the metamorphosis file is moved, not edited, into its own name
(`BarStrip.qml`, `NotificationRow.qml`, ...) in the same commit, so the
diff shows the split.

**P3 Wingpanel bar.** `Surfaces/Bar/BarWingpanel.qml`: no strip card, no
hairline, cells as wingpanel indicators (`0.25rem` margin, `0 0.5rem`
padding, radius 3, the open one filling `highlight` 0.6). The paint comes
from `ThemeEngine.barPaint`, one of `light`, `dark`, `translucentLight`,
`translucentDark`, `maximized`, computed off the wallpaper band under the
bar (mean luminance, standard deviation, acutance, wingpanel's thresholds
180, 45, 8 and the 1.645 sigma rule) plus Hyprland's fullscreen state, and
published in `debug dump` as `bar.paint` with the three numbers. The
sampling reads the wallpaper file `ThemeEngine` already holds, downscaled,
never the screen.

**P4 Popover emerge.** `Drawer` grows `emerge: "popover"`: `joined: false`,
a `card` box with a `menu`-shaped frame, Gala's 150ms menu map (fade plus
a `Theme.space.md` travel out of the cell), no shoulders, no gap
published. The Panel, the centre, the launcher, polkit, the OSD and a
plugin overlay all read the habit; nothing else in them changes.

**P5 Bubble notifications.** `Surfaces/Notifications/NotificationBubble.qml`:
332 wide, radius 9, `card` box, icon left with a 6px gap, bold title, body
at 33 characters, round close button on hover, the 400ms flip-in (opacity
plus an x rotation from 90 through -10 at 60% to 0), restack on
`emphasizedDecel` 200ms with a 150/n stagger. Timeouts and the pending tier
are the service's and do not move. `notifications.sound` (default false)
plays through `canberra-gtk-play` when on PATH, with the honest fallback
of silence.

**P6 Grid default.** `launcher: "grid"` makes `menu.appGrid` default true
under pantheon and nothing else; the grid exists (M58).

**P7 Window chrome.** `chrome.js` grows `$border`, `$shadow`, `$shadowRange`,
`$shadowPower`, `$shadowOffset`, `$shadowColor` from the table's `window`
role, published into `formalshell-chrome.conf`; metamorphosis publishes
`$shadow = false`. The dotfiles' Hyprland decoration block reads them
(M60 Task 7 edits `users/kyandesutter/mixins/hyprland.nix` in
`~/.config/nix`, where `fsRadius` is hardcoded today).

**P8 The switcher.** `Surfaces/Switcher/Switcher.qml` over a `switcher` IPC
target (`next`, `prev`, `commit`, `cancel`, `state`), Gala's numbers from
the spec, drawn from the compositor backend's window list, keyboard only.
The Hyprland bind (`Alt+Tab`) lands in the dotfiles with Task 7.

**P9 Dark schedule.** `theme.mode: "auto"`: sunrise and sunset from the
Weather module's coordinates through the NOAA formula (elementary's
`SunriseSunsetCalculator`), 20:00 to 06:00 without a location, checked
once a minute, flipping `ThemeEngine`'s mode the way `theme mode toggle`
does.

## Evidence

Filled per task.

`--panel-morph` was failing on a different sampled frame each run, and the
bisect cleared every revision: `6ab8b18`, the pre-M59 parity reference, fails
it identically (one pass and one fail in two runs) and `ca1c7b6` both fails
and passes, so no revision broke it. The card's translucent fill lifts the
rig's near-black desktop by about 5%, so the leg's `-threshold 8%` read the
card's own body as bare desktop and left only its 1px bottom border clearing
`panel_morph_body`: a card resting on a fractional row splits that line across
two pixel rows at half strength, the far edge falls back 80 rows into the card,
and the card's contents then read as painted outside it. The cut is
`panel_morph_lift` at 2% now, under the fill's own lift, so the rows a card
covers are what gets measured; green twice at `005f6ba` under metamorphosis and
once under `--pantheon`.

The launcher's app grid came back scrolled off its own card (`scrollTop`
-397 on this rig) because `AppGridView`'s footer reveal was queued with the
delegate that had just taken the cursor and read a tick later: a query that
rebuilds the footer inside that tick leaves `mapToItem` answering as though
the row sat at the scene's own origin, and the unclamped write put the
grid's own offset down the output into `contentY`. The join's clip hid it,
the popover cuts nothing at the card's edge and drew it. The reveal now
resolves the row off the live cursor and scrolls through `cursor.js`'s own
`follow`, so a view with nothing to scroll stays put; `--app-grid` asserts
`scrollTop` 0 across the arrows and its pantheon branch is back to a pixel
read, a cell-sized probe icon with none of its colour above the card's top
row.

M62 is the follow-up Task 3 left open: on the owner's hosts the bar is a left
band inside a screen frame, and a framed bar hands its band to `FrameRing`,
which was painting the `frame` role's card fill, so wingpanel's habit was
invisible on the config that shows the most of it. The ring now carries the
band's own five paints off the same sampler, the two translucent paints'
ink shadows were corrected to elementary's own lighter pair and its none,
`bar.paint` pins the reading (`transparent` keeps the ink adaptive and drops
the wash, which is what the owner's busy forest wallpaper needed), and the
pantheon panels and bubbles went opaque, their namespaces having no blur
behind them. `--frame-adaptive` is the leg.

  `worstLevel` for the bar cell, `pickActive(devices, lastConnectedKey)`.

  `normalise(status)` and `command(controlKey, value)` returning the
  librepods verb (the existing allow-list). Delete `shell/Airpods/`.

  kept) owning backend objects under `shell/Services/Earbuds/`

  `select(key)`, `set(controlKey, value)`.
- `EarbudsPanel.qml` replaces `AirpodsPanel.qml`: renders `controls`
  generically, keyboard cursor keyed by control key, same honest states.
- `EarbudsWidget.qml`, `EarbudsIpc.qml` (target `earbuds`: `status`,
  `devices`, `select`, `set`), registrations in `shell.qml`, `Bar.qml`,
  `Bar/panels.js`, `Bar/layout.js`, `Menu/providers.js`, `Menu/icons.js`,
  `Services/qmldir`; `bar.widgets.earbuds.showLabel`.
- Tests: `tst_earbuds_model.qml`, `tst_earbuds_airpods.qml` (the old
  fixtures moved), `tst_bar_layout.qml`, `tst_menu_reachability.qml`.

- Verify: `dev/vm-lock.sh just vm-test`, `just vm-lint`, `rg -i airpods

# R2: the bar

Goal: the Rust bar draws and behaves like the QML bar, every cell, every
edge, every theme, with the service crates wired into the runtime.

Facts this plan rests on (checked 2026-10-06):

- R1 is merged: runtime, config, state, `fs-theme` and the theme engine,
  IPC with goldens, the Hyprland backend. Six legs pass under
  `FS_IMPL=rust`.
- Pure logic is ported (`fs-chrome` bar layout, workspaces, drawer,
  dither; `fs-info`, `fs-system`, `fs-devices`, `fs-media`). Service
  crates merged: `fs-notifd`, `fs-mpris`, `fs-tray`, `fs-bluez`.
  `fs-upower`, `fs-network`, `fs-audio`, `fs-auth` are in flight.
- The QML bar is `shell/Surfaces/Bar/` (Bar, BarStrip, BarWingpanel,
  BarOverflow, TrayMenu, TrayOverflow) and 30 cells under `widgets/`.
- `QmlModule.qml` and `PluginBarModule.qml` run user QML. The spec's
  "User code" section replaces them with command plugins (Task 5).

Task 1 comes first. Tasks 2 to 6 then run in parallel worktrees, each
owning its own cells and service modules.

## Task 1: bar framework

- Regions and cell order from `fs-chrome`'s layout, `bar.modules`,
  budgets and hidden cells (`bar room`), the chevron and its second bar,
  the marquee (damaging only its own rect, and its clock stops when the
  text fits), every edge, the frame ring, the pantheon wingpanel band with
  its adaptive paint, fullscreen auto-hide.
- A cell kit: Lucide/Nerd icon glyphs through `Icon` names, label, badge,
  active fill, hover and press, tooltips' anchor, the dim unavailable
  cell.
- IPC: `bar` and `debug` verbs these legs read.
- Legs under `FS_IMPL=rust`: `--bar-layout`, `--bar-room`,
  `--bar-title`, `--bar-position <edge>` (all four), `--chevron`,
  `--chevron-quiet`, `--frame`, `--bar-adaptive`, `--fullscreen`, each
  also under `--pantheon` where the leg supports it.

## Task 2: audio, network, Bluetooth, power cells

- Wire `fs-audio`, `fs-network`, `fs-bluez`, `fs-upower` as services.
- Cells: Audio (wheel steps volume), Mic, Network, Bluetooth, Battery,
  Earbuds, Display, Dualsense.
- Legs: `--mic`, `--wheel` (bar half), `--osd` once R5 has the pill
  (skip here), `--overnight` (bar glyph).

## Task 3: tray

- Wire `fs-tray`. Tray and TrayCell, the shell-owned menu, the overflow
  second bar, `tray.maxVisible`.
- Legs: `--tray`, `--tray-overflow`.

## Task 4: media cells

- Wire `fs-mpris` and the radio/airplay/iphone media sources as far as
  the bar reads them. NowPlaying (marquee, budget), Visualizer with its
  cava child owned by playback.
- Legs: `--visualizer`, the bar half of `--media`.

## Task 5: info cells

- Clock (formats), Weather, Github, Usage, SystemUpdate, Tailscale,
  KeyboardLayout, Monitor, Indicators, Bell, ActiveWindow, Launcher,
  CommandModule (every failure path), command plugins per the spec's
  "User code" section (`--plugins` rewritten for an executable fixture),
  Iphone, the caffeinate and
  recording indicators.
- Legs: `--plugins`, `--systemupdate`, `--monitor` (bar half), `--caffeinate`,
  `--bar-layout`'s CommandModule paths.

## Task 6: workspaces and Spaces

- The Spaces cell: chips with ordinals and window icons, the travelling
  pill, herdr badges (the spinner damaging only its rect), the wheel.
  The hover peek preview waits for R8's thumbnails.
- Legs: `--workspaces`, the cell half of `--spaces`.
